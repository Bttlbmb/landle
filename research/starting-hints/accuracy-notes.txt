Wording and classification
Landlocked does not mean no coastline: Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan and Turkmenistan have Caspian shores. 'Has no ocean coastline' is accurate. The UN list independently confirms all 32 developing landlocked countries. Its list does not include the 12 other European countries, so it cannot be cited alone as proof of all 44 records.
https://www.un.org/en/landlocked/about-landlocked-developing-countries
The UN M49 hierarchy independently covers all 195 records. Its regional scheme differs from mledoze for Cyprus (Western Asia vs Europe), South Sudan (Eastern Africa vs Middle Africa), Mexico and some European subdivisions. Regional clues must identify the game convention, not imply there is a single universal continental/subregional scheme.
Name hints use the game's displayed names, including DR Congo, Ivory Coast, Cape Verde and Türkiye. Decompose/ignore diacritics for letter identity. Spaces and hyphens are not letters. A hyphenated name is not two space-separated words. A/E/I/O/U defines vowels. Do not use the rejected alphabetic first/last comparison or 'No letter appears twice'.

Limitations
The independent aggregate neighbour comparison is a country-by-country corroboration exercise, not 195 separately researched national boundary histories. Sensitive or ambiguous count/comparison hints are omitted. World-map PDF fetches returned 403, so their URLs cannot alone justify claiming that a visual audit was completed. Any further manual sea/coast classification needs its own primary evidence and semantic inspection. Review the final bank again after new geographic groups are added.
