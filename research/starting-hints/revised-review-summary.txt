Changes
- Gameplay uses all eligible reviewed alternatives, with roughly 25% name, 25% numerical and 50% geography selection.
- Four examples per country remain one name, one population/land-area and two distinct, non-nested geography facts. Identical example sets fell from 18 groups covering 42 countries to three pairs.
- Neighbour-size groups include all supported positives from the permitted border graph. A neighbour's own ambiguous border count no longer discards its known area comparison. All-smaller is reachable for 11 countries, including Indonesia, Iran and Saudi Arabia.
- Suriname's adjoining French Guiana versus sovereign-France area scope remains conservative. Broad one-larger-neighbour wording is omitted because its upper bound exceeds the breadth cap.
- Hidden neighbour-continent and Indian Ocean-system conventions, the programme-only Pacific island list, and duplicate Caribbean shortlists are excluded from play.
- Pacific coast wording explicitly includes its seas. American wording names North/Central America and the Caribbean; the South/West Asia union avoids a surprising narrow Iran label. Cyprus's revealed Asia label agrees with the hints.
- Atlantic upper bounds include reviewed marginal-sea and overseas scopes without creating unreviewed direct-coast assignments.
- Every round saves immutable previous-answer exclusions. Visible hints, browser tools and Help use that same available answer pool. Guesses and shared-tab resume preserve the hint.
- Every hint needs at least four confirmed matches and no more than 70% declared possible matches. Medium/Hard require at least seven possible answers, including uncertain extras. Both complete and partial six-answer loopholes are closed.
